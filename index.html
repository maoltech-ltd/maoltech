NaijUp

Nigeria's finance and money platform. It covers personal finance, NGX stock market data, investment analysis, startups, jobs and crypto, and includes calculators, a portfolio tracker and a community forum.

About us

A Nigerian tech company with a builder's mindset.

Maoltech (registered as MAOL TECH CONCEPT) is a software company focused on web and mobile application development.

Founded by Mujeeb Olagunju, we build and operate our own products — NaijUp (finance), AWOL Energy (solar & e-mobility) and ShareDrive (carpooling) — and bring that same hands-on experience to every client project.

We keep things simple: clear communication, honest timelines, and software that works.
